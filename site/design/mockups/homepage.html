Open infrastructure / Broadcast & media

A shared foundation for dynamic media.

dmfdeploy explores an open, vendor-neutral way to declare, deploy and observe software workloads in media facilities.

01 / Why this exists

Media facilities can be more adaptable.

Media engineering is moving from fixed appliances to software, IP and commodity compute. Deployment, inventory and operational visibility remain fragmented.

dmfdeploy explores an open foundation for declaring intent, deploying workloads and observing facility state.

02 / Architecture

A continuous operations loop.
