Scene notes for the picture-story. Each scene is 1200 x 620 (SVG) with a PNG at 1800 px wide.
Cast, same in every scene:
- The admin: round head, brown hair, smile, orange shirt, dark trousers.
- The machine: a tall, narrow grey server tower with four rack-style slots (each with a status light) and a small square safe door (four bolts, straight handle) set into its lower front. The door is drawn shut in every scene. The vault's state shows only through a padlock icon on the door: a GREEN OPEN padlock (outlined, lifted shackle) means vault open, the platform can run; a RED CLOSED padlock (filled) means vault closed. Same icon size in every scene. Fingerprint and padlock glyphs come from Material Icons (Apache-2.0); see icon-credits.txt. A big gold key with a ROUND head hangs on a peg beside the vault: the live unlock (seal) key. An empty, new machine is drawn with a dashed outline, hollow slots and no lights.
- Padlocked copy (blue box with a padlock): holds TWO keys, a teal recovery key with a square (diamond) head and a small gold seal key copy. It carries a passkey badge. One copy per enrolled passkey. These boxes hang on the MACHINE's left side, or sit beside a snapshot. Never on a device. No teal key is ever drawn outside a padlocked copy.
- Passkey badges: round discs, clearly not keys. Badge A is lavender with the fingerprint glyph. Badge B is peach with a double ring and three PIN dots. A device carries the badge of its passkey; the copy for that passkey on the machine carries the same badge. Two different passkeys have two different badges.
- Devices: a phone (dark, tall) and a laptop. Devices hold a passkey, not a copy and not a seal key. (The hardware key prop is no longer used in any scene.)
- The recovery package: a cream envelope with a small gold key on it. Only a gold key, never a teal key.
Labels are short and appear in the pictures only where listed below. Numbers 1 to 9 in the top-left badge match the deck steps.

00-idea
Left: a running machine with a green open padlock on its safe door. Label at the top: "Opens the vault so the platform can run". To its right, an arrow leads from the machine to three small app gears, each in a small blue square with a tiny green secret ticket (no padlock, no gold key) above it; they stand for the apps that run. The big gold round-headed key hangs beside the vault, labelled "Unlock key". Two padlocked copies hang on the machine's left side: the upper one has badge A, the lower one badge B. Each holds a teal square-headed key and a small gold key. A dashed vertical line separates the two sides. A tiny legend along the bottom right: green open padlock = "Vault open", red closed padlock = "Vault closed" (only in this scene).
Right: label at the top: "Only you, with your passkey: full control". The admin confirms on a large phone. The screen shows a fingerprint and a green tick, and a thumb touches it. The phone carries badge A. The admin also holds the cream envelope with the gold key (the recovery package).
Not drawn: boxes on devices, teal keys outside a box, the gold key inside a device.

01-install
The admin stands behind a desk with a laptop labelled "Installer"; the laptop screen shows placeholder lines and a bar. A dashed cable runs from the laptop to a new, empty machine (dashed outline, hollow slots, no lights, empty vault circle) labelled "Empty machine". A half-full progress bar sits above the cable with a small arrow. The admin holds up a document with a padlock, labelled "Checkpoint backup + passphrase". No keys, copies or passkeys are drawn.

02-first-unlock
A running machine (lights on) with a green open padlock on its safe door. The big gold round-headed key hangs beside the vault. At the top left, a before and after strip: a red closed padlock, an arrow labelled "starts closed", a green open padlock labelled "opens itself". The admin sits in a chair, relaxed, with a thumb up. Label: "Nothing to do". No teal keys, copies or passkeys are drawn.

03-enrol
The admin points at two devices. The phone (first passkey) carries badge A and a green tick. A laptop on a small table (second passkey, as a backup) carries badge B (three PIN dots) and a green tick; its screen shows a plain enrolment checklist with two ticks. An arrow from each device leads to the machine on the right. There, two padlocked copies hang, one per passkey: badge A on the upper copy, badge B on the lower copy. Each holds a teal square-headed key and a small gold key. The machine shows a green open padlock on its safe door and the big gold key hangs beside it. Label under the devices: "2 passkeys". The devices carry no boxes. No hardware key is drawn.

04-package
The admin puts the cream envelope (small gold key on it) into a small safe whose door stands open to the left; the door shows a dial and a handle, and the envelope lies inside the safe. Label above: "You keep it". The safe is on the left of the frame, away from the machine. On the right, faded, the machine stands with a green open padlock on its safe door, the big gold key beside it and its two padlocked copies (badges A and B) on its left side.

05-root-token
Upper left: two step tags, "1 Sign in as admin" and "2 Approve with passkey", so the order reads left to right (tag 2 sits above the phone). Left: the admin holds a large phone. Its screen asks "Approve this change?" and shows a fingerprint (badge-A style) with a green tick; a thumb touches the fingerprint. The phone does not say "Root token" and has no box. Middle: a laptop on a small desk shows a QR code, with the label "scan to approve" under the desk. Right (machine side): a machine with a green open padlock on its safe door. A gear (the executor) is joined to it by an axle. A teal arrow arcs from the phone, clear above the laptop, to the gear, labelled "only for this change" (the key material reaches the machine for this one approved change). At the gear, a small root token ticket is crossed out in red at once, labelled "Root token: used once, gone". An arrow from the gear points up to one green ticket with a clock icon, labelled "Narrow token: one change". A dashed arrow leads to a second, faded ticket crossed out in red, labelled "Used up". Tickets are drawn only on the machine side. The machine's copies are not drawn in this scene.

The big gold key hangs beside the vault, and two padlocked copies (badges A and B) hang on its left side. Label at the bottom: "Restart: opens itself". On the right, a wall calendar headed "30 days" with the first nine days crossed off and no warning marks.

09-lost-device
Left: the admin holds a laptop that carries badge B (three PIN dots), the passkey that stays. Top middle: a phone, faded and tilted, with a faded badge A, a red cross and the label "Lost". Middle: the machine with a green open padlock on its safe door and two padlocked copies on its left side. Both copies hold a teal key and a small gold key. A circular arrow above the big gold key at the vault shows it is renewed. Right: an old cream envelope with a gold key, crossed out, a circular arrow, and a new envelope. Label: "Replaced". The scene says nothing about who approves the removal.
